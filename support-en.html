FAQ

Where is my data stored?

All your data stays on your device; if iCloud is on, it is backed up only to your personal iCloud space. It is never sent to us or any third party. See the Privacy Policy.

Do I need to connect my bank account?

No. The app does not link banks and requires no account. You enter your payments once, and the app reminds you of the rest.

What does Premium unlock?

Premium only unlocks the 12-month view in the Forecast screen (6 months in the free version). Unlimited entries, calendar, and reminders are free. Monthly, yearly, or one-time lifetime options are available; there is no free trial.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone, go to Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions to manage or cancel anytime. See the Terms of Use.
