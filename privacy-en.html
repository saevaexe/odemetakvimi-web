Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 11, 2026

Ödeme Takvimi (the "app") treats privacy as a core design principle, not a feature. This document explains what data is processed and how, in line with Apple App Privacy and Turkey's KVKK requirements. In short: your data stays on your device and (if enabled) in your personal iCloud; it is never sent to us or any third party.

1. Data Controller

This app is published by Osman Seven as an individual developer.

  • Contact: osman.seven97@icloud.com
  • Bundle ID: com.osmanseven.odemetakvimi

2. Data We Collect

We do not transfer any personal data to our servers or to third parties. The app requires no account; it does not ask for registration, email, or phone number.

Data stored on your device

  • The payment records you enter: card statement amounts, installments, loans, bills, subscriptions, and paid markers
  • App preferences (such as reminder settings)

This data is kept in your device's local storage; if iCloud Drive is enabled, the same file is also backed up to your personal iCloud space so you don't lose your data when changing devices. That backup belongs to your Apple account — no one, including the developer, can access it. If iCloud is off, the app keeps working on-device only. It also works without an internet connection.

No bank connection

The app does not connect to your bank account, does not read your SMS messages, and does not ask for your card number. It only stores identifiers you voluntarily enter (such as the last 4 digits) on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your personal backup.

3. Purchases

Premium subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed entirely through the Apple App Store. We have no access to your payment information. The app uses only Apple's StoreKit framework to verify purchase status.

4. Notifications

Payment reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification server is used; notification content never leaves your device. If you do not grant notification permission, the app will not schedule reminders.

5. Analytics and Tracking

  • There is no third-party analytics SDK.
  • There is no advertising or advertising identifier (IDFA) tracking.
  • Apple's optional, anonymous app analytics is processed by Apple only if you have allowed it in iOS settings.

6. Deleting Your Data

You can permanently delete all your data from within the app via Settings → Reset all data. Removing the app also deletes all app data on the device. Since we do not hold your data, no separate deletion request is needed.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect data from children; in fact, it does not collect data from any user.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page and the effective date is updated.

9. Contact

For questions: osman.seven97@icloud.com
